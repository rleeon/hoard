关于 Hoard

三种长度，可直接粘贴。原样使用或作为起点都可以。

一句话

Hoard 是一款免费开源应用，可备份游戏存档，并在多台设备之间保持同步，保留每一个版本。

简短

Hoard 会自动备份游戏存档，并在 Windows、macOS、Linux 和 Steam Deck 之间同步：在一台机器上停下，游戏就在下一台机器上等着。每次改动都会保存为可以回退的版本。无论游戏来自哪个商店，它都能自动找到数千款游戏和模拟器的存档。可以使用托管在欧盟的免费 Hoard One，也可以在自己的硬件上运行同一个开源服务器。

完整

Hoard 是一款免费开源工具，用于备份和同步 PC 游戏存档。一个小巧的后台服务会监视游戏保存存档的文件夹；每次游戏结束时，它会把改动保存为一个新版本，并同步到玩家的其他机器上，支持 Windows、macOS、Linux 和 Steam Deck。无论游戏来自哪个商店，数千款游戏的存档以及模拟器、Proton 或 Wine 前缀中的存档都会被自动检测，任何文件夹也都可以手动添加。版本按内容哈希去重，因此一个 2 GB 存档的十个版本只占约 2 GB，而不是 20 GB，并且每个文件都会用 SHA-256 校验。使用方式有两种。Hoard One 是托管选项，服务器位于欧盟，提供免费方案。自托管则在自有硬件上运行同一个服务器，从 NAS 到树莓派都可以，无需 Hoard 账号，没有配额，也不会向项目发送任何数据。Hoard 使用 Rust 编写，采用 AGPL-3.0 许可证，由独立开发者 Raimundo León Oliva 开发。

最新版本

v1.3.0 · 2026年10月25日

更新说明以英文撰写。

Hoard Cloud becomes Hoard One and Pro can be bought as a single year. The Steam Deck gets a Game Mode plugin, arrow-key navigation and detection for games added to Steam as shortcuts. Uploads get faster and lighter, one file can be pulled out of any old version without restoring it, and sync keeps running on Linux machines that nobody has logged in to.

新增

  • · A Steam Deck plugin for Game Mode.
  • · A year of Pro, paid once.
  • · Download a version without restoring it.
  • · Move around the app with the arrow keys.
  • · Games added to Steam as shortcuts are found.
  • · Halloween, from 24 October to 3 November.
  • · Hoard-help can attach your server's log.
  • · Notifications for downloads from the cloud.

变更

  • · Hoard Cloud is now Hoard One.
  • · Faster, lighter uploads.
  • · Panel cards fit the screen.
  • · Hoard One learns which parts of the app get used.
修复 (9)
  • · "Back up now" did nothing after a run of failures.
  • · The app opened and closed at once on some Linux handhelds.
  • · The AppImage would not open in sandboxes such as firejail.
  • · The app could show nothing in the Steam Deck's Game Mode.
  • · The app sometimes switched to the system language.
  • · Sync stayed off on Linux machines where nobody had logged in.
  • · Changing a setting in History could freeze the window for a minute.
  • · Pro could drop to Free for a moment at each renewal.
  • · "Change plan" could start a second Pro subscription.
